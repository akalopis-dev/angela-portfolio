What I did

  • Co-President of the DECA chapter, now in its second year: chapter operations, event selection, competition prep and mock role-plays.
  • This year 100% of our DECA members wrote the regional exam, up from only a few last year.
  • Co-President of the school’s first-year FBLA chapter of about 50 members.
  • Working toward an independent case competition with Medway and Oakridge.
  • Finance Director at the Canadian Young Investors Society since 2025. Ran FOCUS, an in-person case competition in Toronto in spring 2026 with 50+ students.
  • Working toward an independent case competition with schools in London.
  • Competed at DECA Ontario Provincials 2026 in the Business Finance Series.
  • Won the capstone venture challenge at Ivey’s 2025 Summer Leadership Program, winning first place out of 25 teams, with REPUP, a concept to get Canadians repairing, reusing and upcycling.
  • Top 5 of 34 teams at the 2025 Ivey High School Case Competition, with recommendations for Meals on Wheels London.

In my words

Competing in DECA is about you and your event. Running a chapter is about everyone else: registrations, practice, deadlines, and getting every member to write the regional exam.

I found out that is the part I like. I want to know how the whole chapter runs, so when someone has a question, I am the person who can answer it. It has made me much better at managing my time.

DECA is where I get to work on something I care about and pull other people into it.
