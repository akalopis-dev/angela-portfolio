In my words

Running taught me that training only gets you to the start line. On race day, you have to want it.

I learned that at our school board meet, which decided who moved on to the Western Ontario championship. One of our runners, who had been injured all season, dropped out mid-race. Suddenly my result was the one that counted for the team score, and I had to lock in.

Now I train for my team as much as for myself. Run for something greater than yourself.
