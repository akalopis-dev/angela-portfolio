What I did

  • Won the Diamond Challenge Toronto Social Innovation competition with My Volunteer Pathway.
  • Selected as a Top 40 global team from 3,000+ teams in 100+ countries, run by Horn Entrepreneurship at the University of Delaware.
  • One of two Canadian teams in the Social Innovation track.
  • Competed internationally as a finalist at the Limitless World Summit in Delaware in April 2026.
  • Top 10 finalist at the Canada Young Changemakers Summit, one of 10 teams invited to pitch in person from 60+ applicants.
  • Ingenious+ Regional Winner from the Rideau Hall Foundation, presented at MaRS in Toronto with $1,000 in award funding.
02

In my words

I built My Volunteer Pathway for an Ontario problem. Every student needs 40 volunteer hours to graduate, and a lot of those hours get lost on paper sheets.

At the Limitless World Summit, most of the judges and students had never heard of that rule. I had to rethink my pitch for students in other countries, like National Honor Society members in the US, who also need service hours.

The part that meant the most to me was the people. I met ambitious students from almost every part of the world, all building something of their own.
