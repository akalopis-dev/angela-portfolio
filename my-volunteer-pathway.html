01

The problem

Many organizations rely on disconnected systems for recruiting volunteers, managing applications, scheduling shifts, tracking attendance and verifying hours. Students still carry paper hour sheets that get lost. At the same time, young people struggle to find opportunities that match their interests and availability.

I noticed it myself. I missed out on volunteer opportunities, and I kept forgetting my hours sheet, so hours I had worked never got documented.

In my first period class last year, a Grade 12 class, half the students were handed slips saying they were missing volunteer hours. Many of them had volunteered. They just never got their sheets signed.

02

What I did

  • Developed the idea and overall strategy.
  • Designed the product, the brand, the pitch deck and the brochures.
  • Contacted 400+ London-area organizations, ran demos and onboarded them.
  • Took 40+ calls booked through my Calendly, from product demos to advice and mentoring sessions.
  • Recruited and lead a team of student outreach members and ambassadors.
  • Built the pricing model: 30 days free, then $19 CAD a month, $49 CAD a month for organizations with 25+ volunteers, and custom pricing for institutions.
  • Turned organization feedback into product changes.
  • Represented the platform at community events and in entrepreneurship competitions.
03

How I built it

I built My Volunteer Pathway myself, starting in November 2025, using AI coding tools, and I develop it today. I also run design, outreach, pricing and partnerships. My dad, who has a technical background, mentors me.

I redesigned the interface around a Digital Volunteer Passport, using the look of a travel document instead of a generic software dashboard, so a student’s record of hours feels like something worth keeping.

I started coding in 2020 at Code Ninjas and earned my Black Belt, the first girl in Canada to reach Black Belt level. My final project was Horse Racing Heroes, a racing game.

Results

As of November 4, 2026: 27 organizations and 100+ volunteers registered. Organizations on the platform span youth programs, health, disability advocacy, wellness, fundraising, community service and recreation in London and the surrounding area.

The platform supplied volunteers for a Porsche Club event at London International Airport and generated applicants for other community roles.

Accepted into the 2026 Ontario Summer Company program through Small Business Centre London, with a $3,000 grant, mentorship and training.
