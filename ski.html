In my words

Every kid learns differently and starts at a different level. I learned to watch how each skier reacted to my cues, then change the lesson until it worked for them.

I had one of the heaviest lesson loads on staff: four Skee Bees group lessons for ages 5 to 6, then four Junior Ski group lessons on Sunday for ages 7 to 13, back to back. A single group could have five kids at five different levels.

Ski instructing taught me how to manage chaos. It is also a lot of fun.
